CARNAC OS is a dual-timbral wavetable synth drawing inspiration from neolithic monuments and forgotten rituals. Designed for deep sound design exploration, it combines a MAQ 16/3-style sequencer for intricate modulated sequences with a chord sequencer for harmonic progressions.

Install Carnac 0.9.6From Chrome or Edge on a computer, with the FM1 connected by USB. It updates from stock firmware or from any earlier Carnac, keeps your saved patches, and writes the PCM waves. ManualThe panel, voices and layers, oscillators and filters, modulation, effects, the sequencer and arpeggiator, presets, and what changed in each version. Button guideWhat every button on the FM1 does, drawn in the screen's own runes and stones, in any of its themes. Play it in the browserThe same synth engine, with sound: mouse, touch or keyboard, no FM1 needed. Download carnac-0.9.6.fwscFor the command-line installer: python3 tools/fm1_install.py carnac-0.9.6.fwsc. InspirationAbout a decade ago I got into this really satisfying live music making vibe involving a tape recorder, a couple of synths, sequencers and a mixer. It's probably the most fun I ever had recording. I wasn't worried about producing, just diving deep into the textures. This synth is inspired by that sound and process. SourceGitHub, GPL-3.0.
