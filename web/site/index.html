Synthesizer firmware for the M-VAVE FM1: wavetable and LA-style voices, two layers, filters, effects, a sequencer and an arpeggiator.

Install Carnac __VERSION__From Chrome or Edge on a computer, with the FM1 connected by USB. It updates from stock firmware or from any earlier Carnac, keeps your saved patches, and writes the PCM waves. ManualThe panel, voices and layers, oscillators and filters, modulation, effects, the sequencer and arpeggiator, presets, and what changed in each version. __EMU__ Download __PKG__For the command-line installer: python3 tools/fm1_install.py __PKG__. The PCM waves are firmware/pcm_area0.bin and pcm_area1.bin beside it, for src/hardware/pcm_store_upload.py. __TIP__ SourceGitHub, GPL-3.0. Built on X0X and Felucca.

Installing is at your own risk. To go back to M-VAVE's firmware, use the last section of the installer, or M-VAVE's own M-UPGRADE from m-vave.com/download. M-VAVE and FM-1 are trademarks of their owners.
