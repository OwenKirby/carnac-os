in the browser: the same code the FM1 runs

InstallSource

Turn your phone sideways for bigger controls.

Off. Sound starts when you switch it on.

MOUSE AND TOUCH

  • Drag a knob up or down, or use the scroll wheel over it.
  • Click or touch buttons and keys. Several fingers at once work on a touch screen.
  • Chords with a mouse: Shift-click (or right-click) a button to hold it down, click again to let go. Hold HOME this way and press OCT− / OCT+ to edit layer 1 / layer 2.

KEYBOARD

  • White keys 1–8: Q–I, 9–16: A–K. Black keys 1–11: 1–0, -.
  • Space PLAY, Enter REC, Shift SEL (held), Esc HOME, Z / X OCT− / OCT+.
  • ↑ ↓ turn SELECT, ← → turn ALGORITHM.
  • MIDI IN plays it from a MIDI keyboard, as the FM1's USB port does.

SAVING

SAVE writes to the FM1's flash, which this browser keeps: your patches, performances, sequences, arps and GLOBAL settings are still there next time. FACTORY RESET forgets them. It is the firmware, not a recording: what you hear is Carnac running, at the FM1's 44.1 kHz, on one core. The idea of running FM1 firmware in the browser, and this page's shape, come from X0X's emulator, after Groove OS's.
