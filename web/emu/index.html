Menu

Section buttons from the FM1 spec (2.1), sent to the core as the FM1's buttons are: a press opens the section's first page, the next press cycles its submenus (skipping the ones the layer's architecture hides). SAVE: the first press opens PRESET, the next SAVE. There, ACTION picks what a SAVE press runs: SAVE asks ARE YOU SURE?, or OVERWRITE? when the slot already holds a save (ALGORITHM picks, SAVE confirms); COPY puts the TARGET's live item on the clipboard; PASTE (offered once the clipboard holds that kind) asks, then replaces the live item. The emulator has no flash: saves stay in memory until the page reloads. Any button or encoder clears SAVED. HOME acts when released. Hold it and press OCT− / OCT+ to edit LAYER 1 (wood) / LAYER 2 (stone) instead: the page you are on stays up, and HOME does nothing on release. While HOME is held the focused layer's OCT button is lit and the other flashes. The whole screen wears the focused layer's colours: oak for LAYER 1, granite for LAYER 2.

Screen

Colours follow the layer being edited: oak for LAYER 1, granite for LAYER 2.

Every page, drawn by the firmware's own renderers (src/ui_page_draw.c, src/ui_seq_draw.c, src/ui_arp_draw.c) inside the core. The screen's text uses your system font; the FM1 uses Inter Tight.

Sequencer

PLAY (or Space) starts and stops the MAQ sequencer from any page; navigating never stops it. On the SEQ page the 16 white keys (marked lane:step) are four blocks of four: C D E F lane 1, G A B C lane 2, D E F G lane 3, A B C D lane 4 (each block's first key glows; the active lane and step group's key is lit), and a key's place in its block puts steps 1–4, 5–8, 9–12 or 13–16 of that lane on the knobs. The black keys do nothing there. Hold OCT− or OCT+ (or Z / X) and press white key n to set the active lane's loop start / end to step n: while held, the 16 keys show the lane's steps (the loop window glows, the playhead is lit, the boundary you are about to move is lit when stopped and half lit when playing). REC (SEQ page) enters step record: SELECT walks the steps, knob 1 to 4 edit lanes 1 to 4, and keys (held together for a chord) write the step (its notes light on the keyboard). Pick the lane and its EV / TARGET / MD / P with SELECT and ALGORITHM; TARGET hides when it has one choice. Tempo: HOME (last page) → GLOBAL → knob 4 (or the INT TEMPO row), with CLOCK SOURCE on INTERNAL.

ARP (spec 8): the 13 keys C–C (marked underneath) are chord keys: each plays a 4-voice chord from the GENRE BANK. The 8 white keys above them are step keys 1–8 (marked on top); steps up to SEQ LENGTH glow (rests stay dark), the playing step's key is lit, and while playing the chord key of the transposition is half lit. Hold step keys and strike a chord key to capture that chord into them, with the four knobs (GATE, OCTAVE, STYLE, RHYTHM) as they are; a knob turned while step keys are held edits those steps. PLAY runs the pattern with the MAQ; while it plays, a chord key transposes it by its distance from the first chord key (and the MAQ too with TRANSPOSE MAQ on). The black keys among the step keys are functions (marked on top): LAT toggles latch (the chord's arpeggio plays on after you let go); hold TIE, CLR or ACC and tap step keys (or hold step keys and tap them) to make a tie (the chord before carries on), a rest, or toggle the accent (first strike at ACCENT VEL); hold CPY, tap the source step, then the steps to copy it to. REC on the ARP page records: playing, chord keys land on the nearest step and the knobs overdub the playing step; stopped, chord keys fill steps 1, 2, 3… (SEQ LENGTH follows), and TIE / CLR alone record a tie or a rest.

Knobs

Knob 1 to 4 for the page above (2.2), as the layer's voice architecture maps them (set it with L1 ARCH / L2 ARCH on HOME). Drag up or down, scroll, or use the arrow keys on a focused knob. Pitch Bend springs back to centre. Knob 1 to 3 on HOME are sent as MIDI. Greyed knobs have no parameter in this architecture.

Parameters

SELECT
ALGORITHM
PRESET

The page's selectable parameters (2.2), in the core's own order. SELECT moves the cursor down the list (wheel, buttons, or the arrow keys on the encoder); ALGORITHM changes the highlighted value. Greyed rows are skipped. PRESET loads the next or previous saved preset (5.2): a sequencer slot on SEQ, a performance on HOME, an arp pattern on ARP, and the focused layer's patch on every other page. It skips slots never saved, so save something first.
